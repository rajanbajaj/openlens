OpenLens:
search you can own.

Crawl the web, index it, search it — the whole pipeline, on your own machine. No API keys, no black box.

01 · Problem

Web search is a black box.

  • One company's crawler, ranking, and indexResearchers and communities can't inspect or reproduce it
  • Every query API charges per callSearch is rented, never owned
  • No control over what gets indexed — or whyOpaque ranking decides what you see
02 · Solution

Crawl → index → search.
Your hardware. Your rules.

  • Point it at the web — a fleet of crawlers does the rest
  • Search over your own index, with your own ranking rules
  • Open source, runs on one machineNo external search dependency, no tracking — and ranking that protects by construction

05 · Self-healing

One click handles 14,000 dead URLs.

  • 429 rate limit → wait and retryVolume-scaled backoff, per domain
  • Timeouts → the polite backoff poolSlow workers, separate Kafka topic, main drain never stalls
  • robots.txt / 404 / dead servers → skipAuto-triage sorts every failure class
06 · Operator control

Search you can steer.

  • Drill into any domain — every failing URL, and why
  • Requeue, deprioritize, skip, or blockQueued and failed domains, same handles
  • Failures explain themselves, operators decideTransparency all the way down
07 · Why it matters

Ranking that protects.

  • Child-safe by constructionAdult and unsafe content blocked in the tool layer, not the prompt — it can't be jailbroken, and a filter failure blocks the request instead of leaking
  • Stalking-free by designPrivacy filters stop personal-data aggregation queries cold — no tracking profiles, anonymous feedback, secrets redacted
  • Neutral, not engagingNo engagement optimization; popularity never decides contested topics — perspectives surface the sides others hide

And the guarantee is self-hosting: safety isn't a vendor's policy promise, it's code you can audit running on hardware you own. Anyone can promise safety. We ship it.

11 · What's next

The trajectory, not just the snapshot.

  • Stay fresh — recrawl loops, not recrawlsSitemaps + RSS drive continuous refresh; a search engine that rots is an archive
  • Earn trust — reputation scoringFeedback becomes site reputation + fake detection; neutrality shows all sides, reputation shows who's credible
  • Scale out — from fleet to elasticAutoscaling on Kafka lag, real connection pooling, multi-node frontier
  • Share indexes — borrow, don't recrawlResearch: voluntarily published public indexes you subscribe to and mix into your own ranking — a senior dev's index teaches juniors through her taste. Shared anonymously, so no identity is ever at stake
  • Parental control, finishedChild-safe grows from a mode into a profile: per-kid allowlists, time-aware rules, and a parent dashboard showing what was blocked and why
  • Search anywhere SDKDrop OpenLens into your own site, then index beyond pages: local files, docs, video transcripts, audio — one ranking pipeline over everything you own
