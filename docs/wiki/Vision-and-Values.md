# Vision and Values (product)

OpenLens is search you can inspect: your own crawl, your own
ranking, every decision explained.

- **Neutrality over engagement**: ranking never uses gender
  stereotypes, popularity never decides contested topics, and
  [Perspectives](Perspectives) shows missing sides instead of
  hiding them.
- **Safety by construction**: child-safe filtering, adult/unsafe
  blocks inside the tool layer (not the prompt), privacy filters
  against personal-data aggregation and stalking queries.
- **Transparency**: why-ranked reasons, trust warnings, labelled
  sponsorship, model attribution (`Powered by …`), and honest
  fallbacks that say what they are.
- **Your data, your index**: no external search dependency, no
  tracking profiles, anonymous feedback, redacted secrets.

Non-goals: maps, general web-Scale crawling, and any feature that
needs a third-party search API.
